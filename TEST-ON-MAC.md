# Try Stillpoint on your Mac

## Start

1. Put on headphones and turn your Mac's volume down low.
2. In Finder, open the project folder and double-click **Test Stillpoint on Mac.command**.
   (The first time, macOS may say it can't check the file: right-click it, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.)
3. Safari opens Stillpoint. Setup is two short pages; on the second, click **Play a sample** and set the volume.

To stop everything, close the Terminal window that opened.

## Controls

In a session:

- **Middle button:** pause and play.
- **Left button (square):** stop. It asks first.
- **Right button (circle arrow):** start again from the beginning.

The slider sets the volume. When a session ends, **Play again** starts it over.

## What to try

1. **Inner Coherence › Steady Heart** (10 min). Breathe in as the wind rises and out as it settles, in a forest with distant birds. At about 4 minutes the voice becomes softer and slower.
2. **Sleep › Steady Heart for Sleep.** The same breathing, then it drifts into sleep. Listen to the first 15 minutes or so; the voice simply stops.
3. **Quit Smoking › Craving now?** on the home screen (2 minutes), to hear the clear voice.
