Use Stillpoint over HTTPS

Optional. Over HTTPS the Home Screen app works offline (history, settings, and sessions you have already played with the live engine) and can share your data export with the share sheet. Everything else works the same over plain HTTP.

1. Install this Mac’s certificate

Tap the link below in Safari, then choose Allow. iPhone downloads it as a profile.

Download certificate

It was created on your Mac by serve-web.sh and only that Mac holds its private key. It lets your phone trust the Mac’s local web server and nothing else you’d otherwise visit. You can remove it any time in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.

2. Install the profile

Open Settings. Tap “Profile Downloaded” near the top (or General › VPN & Device Management), then Install.

3. Trust it

Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings. Turn on “Stillpoint local development CA”.

4. Open the secure address

Open Stillpoint over HTTPS

Then Share › Add to Home Screen. Your history in the plain-HTTP version stays there; export and import isn’t built, so start fresh or keep using one address.
