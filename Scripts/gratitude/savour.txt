# Gratitude - "Three Good Things" (~10 min)
# voice: af_heart*0.65+af_nicole*0.35
# speed: 0.9
# gap: 0.9
# soft: on

[gr-pre-1]
Welcome. For the next few minutes, we are going to slow down and notice a few good things. They do not have to be big. And if nothing comes to mind at some point, that is completely fine. Just rest, and listen.

[gr-ind-1]
Let your eyes close, and take a slow breath in. And let it go, a little longer on the way out.

[gr-ind-2]
Again, breathe in gently. And breathe out, letting your shoulders soften and your hands rest.

[gr-ind-3]
Let your breathing find its own rhythm now. Feel the weight of your body, supported. With each breath out, your mind becomes a little quieter, a little more spacious.

[gr-one-1]
Now, let your mind drift back over today, or yesterday. See if you can find one small good thing. It might be a warm drink. A moment of sunlight. A kind word. Something that went a little better than expected. Take your time. I will be quiet for a few moments while you look.

[gr-one-2]
Whatever you have found, bring it closer. Where were you? What could you see, or hear, or taste? Let the moment become vivid again, as if it is happening now.

[gr-one-3]
Notice how it feels in your body to remember this. Perhaps a little warmth, or a softening somewhere. Let yourself enjoy it for a few more breaths.

[gr-two-1]
Now, bring to mind a person who has helped you, or cared about you. It could be someone from long ago, or someone you saw this week. Let their face appear in your mind.

[gr-two-2]
Remember something they did for you. It may have cost them time, or effort, or thought. They did not have to do it. And they did.

[gr-two-3]
Silently, in your own words, thank them. Imagine that they can hear you, and notice how they might look as they receive it. I will give you a quiet moment for this.

[gr-three-1]
And now, think of one thing in your life that you rely on. Something so familiar that you rarely notice it. A place to sleep. A friend. Your eyesight. Clean water from a tap.

[gr-three-2]
For just a moment, imagine your life without it. Notice what would be missing.

[gr-three-3]
And now, come back. It is here. You have it. Let that land, as a quiet sense of good fortune.

[gr-int-1]
Being grateful does not mean pretending that everything is fine. It simply means that, alongside whatever is hard, you can also see what is good. Both can be true.

[gr-int-2]
Over the coming days, you may find that you notice small good things a little more easily. A taste. A kindness. A moment of ease. And each time, you can pause, for one breath, and take it in.

[gr-em-1]
Now it is time to return. Take a deeper breath in, and feel energy coming back into your body.

[gr-em-2]
Stretch your fingers and your toes. Let your breathing become brighter and fuller.

[gr-em-3]
And when you are ready, open your eyes. Feeling awake, alert, and quietly glad. If you like, write down one thing you noticed before you carry on with your day.
